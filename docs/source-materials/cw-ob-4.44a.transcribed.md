# Commonwealth Order of Battle (§4.44a OA-Sheets) — Transcription

**Source:** *The Campaign for North Africa* (rulebook PDF), §4.44a Commonwealth OA-sheet charts.
**Commonwealth OB pages in this transcription:** PDF p118 – p132.
**Note on section start:** Page 118 opens mid-section — its first chart (8th Armored Division) is already a British/Commonwealth armored division, so the Commonwealth/British OB begins on a page *before* 118 (earlier British formations such as the 1st/7th Armored Divisions were presumably on prior pages outside the requested range). Page 132 ends cleanly with "Unassigned Allied Units," which is the end of the Commonwealth OB section.
**Total formations transcribed:** 35 (32 with unit tables + 3 prose entries).
**Sub-groupings within the section:** British/Commonwealth divisions & unassigned pools (p118–124), Australian (p124–125), Indian (p126–128), New Zealand (p129), South African (p129–131), Allies/Free French/Greek/Polish (p131–132).

**Column key:** ID = ID Code (1–2 letter lowercase, transcribed verbatim). Arrives: a bare "D" = Deployed. "N" in TOE column = as printed (no explicit weapon system).

**Legibility verdict:** Overall very legible. Minor low-confidence cells (best guess given, marked "(?)"):
- 44th Infantry Div / 44th Recce Regt / ID — read as `ll` (lowercase double-L, the standard recce code); could be misread as `II`. (?)
- All recce-regiment ID codes rendered `ll` follow the same lowercase double-L convention (50th, 51st, 6th/7th/9th Aus Cav, 2nd NZ Cav, Central India Horse, 13th DCO/DCL) — verbatim `ll`.
- 1st South African Inf Div / 3rd SA Field Artillery Regt (THA) footnote is printed as `dd = THA stands for...` but references note letter `d` in the table — printed as `dd` in the notes block (likely a typo for `d`). (?)
- South African / "2nd S. African Police" counter abbreviation prints as `1 SA Plc` (same as 1st S. African Police) — appears to be a source typo; transcribed as printed. (?)
- 1st Free French Bde / 1st Regt d'artillerie: counter abbreviation prints as a bare `1` (rest of cell blank). (?)

## Selby Force / The Matruh Garrison (PROSE) — PDF p121
Basic Morale: None (that of attached units). **ID Code: a**
Brigade-level HQ present mostly for administrative purposes; the Mersa Matruh Garrison HQ at campaign start. No units assigned inherently (up to six may be attached as garrison units for Mersa Matruh). Deployed on 1/1; may not move voluntarily until it becomes the Selby Force (Operations Stage 2 of Game-Turn 12) — the only Commonwealth Battle Group — after which it moves freely; disbanded during Organization Phase of 3/21.

## 22nd Guards Brigade (200th Guards Brigade) (PROSE) — PDF p121
Basic Morale: None (that of attached units). **ID Code: a**
Infantry brigade HQ with no battalions initially assigned; if attached to an armored division it gains Motor Brigade Group attributes. Underwent multiple redesignations: became 200th Guards Brigade (January 1942), 200th Guards Motor Brigade Group (April 1942), 201st Guards Motor Brigade Group (May 1942). Assigned to the 70th Infantry Division February 1941 (2/21) until April 1941 (1/29). Two counters provided: *22 Gds* and *200 Gds*.
