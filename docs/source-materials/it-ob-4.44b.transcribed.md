Source: `docs/source-materials/The Campaign for North Africa.pdf`, pages 147–161.

- **Italian OB pages:** 147–161 (all pages in range are Italian OB). Page 147 also carries the §4.44b intro paragraphs and the column-legend/abbreviations block (transcribed as the Legend note below, not as a formation).
- **Total formations transcribed:** 35 (33 with unit tables + 2 prose-only sections: "Italian Battle Groups" and the "Italian Blackshirt (CCNN) Units" intro).
- **Requested formations status:**
  - 64 "Catanzaro" Division — PRESENT (p153), transcribed.
  - 132 "Ariete" Armored Division — PRESENT (p147), transcribed.
  - 185 "Folgore" Airborne Division — PRESENT (p154), transcribed.
  - 62 "Marmarica" Division — NOT a separate OA sheet in this range. Referenced only in the Libyan Tank Command notes: `LXII(L) = 62 Marmarica`.
  - 63 "Cirene" Division — NOT a separate OA sheet in this range. Referenced only in the Libyan Tank Command notes: `LXIII(L) = 63 Cirene`.

## Legibility verdict

The scan is generally legible. Cells I could not read with full confidence (best guess marked "(?)"):

- **132 Ariete / I(L) Tank Bn & II(L) Tank Bn / ID + TOE** — both cells are genuinely blank in the source (note e says they are attached, not assigned), not an OCR failure.
- **Gruppo Maletti / 1–3 Sahariano Camel Bn / Counter Abbr** — read as `1Sno/Sno`, `2Sno/Sno`, `3Sno/Sno` (the doubled "Sno" is faint) (?).
- **RECAM (Raggr. Esplorante) / RECAM HQ / TOE** — read as `1×vv` (unusual; the weapon token is faint) (?).
- **Bardia Garrison / 30th Gaf. S. di C. HQ / Counter Abbr** — printed as `32GaF` although the unit is the 30th (apparent source typo); transcribed verbatim (?).
- **60th "Sabratha" Division (p149)** — the chart sits at the very bottom edge of the page image; last visible rows are 60th Engineer Bn and 60th Bersaglieri Mitrg Coy. No Notes block is visible; a trailing note (if any) may have been clipped.
- **The Saharan Detachment (p154)** — chart runs to the bottom edge of the page (last visible row: Meharisiti Camel Cavalry); a trailing Notes block (if any) may have been clipped before the p155 Benghazi chart.
- **17th Pavia / 27 Brescia / several CCNN rows** — a number of HQ/CCNN/tank rows legitimately have blank ID/TOE/Arrives cells in the source; transcribed as blank, not marked (?).

The Vth and VIth were stripped and assigned immediately to the Ariete, and were replaced (later) by the DLIV and DLVI Articelere Battalions.
