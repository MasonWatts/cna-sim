## Result meanings (footnotes)
- **Pinned** — the unit may not Retreat Before Assault and may not participate in Assault.
- **1** — Infantry\* or Armor: lose one TOE Strength Point **and** Pinned. Guns or Trucks:
  lose one TOE Strength Point.
- **2** — as above, except lose **two** points.
- **\*** — if the Infantry is in Trucks, eliminate one Truck Point (transport not yet modeled).
