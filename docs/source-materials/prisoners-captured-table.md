# Prisoners Captured Results Table (Rules §15.89)

Human-readable transcription. Machine data: `data/reference/crt/prisoners.ron`.

Roll one die:

| DIE | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| % Prisoners | 10 | 25 | 33 | 50 | 50 | 75 |

**% Prisoners** = Percentage of Casualties of Infantry, Armor, and Gun losses that are
taken as Prisoners by the opposing Player.
