## Key
- `*` HQ eligible for anti-tank augmentation (Case 19.8). `**` retains CPA regardless of assigned TOE. `+` motorized-capable.
- `‡` when truck-transported the unit counts as being in halftracks (Case 14.88); otherwise it has no Armor Protection.
- `( )` conditional rating (Case 3.4). `-` not applicable.

## Chart notes (verified)
1. From the 1/39 Game-Turn, `ff` reconnaissance units may be upgraded (Case 20.5).
2. From the 1/75 Game-Turn, all German battle groups and certain HQs may be augmented by anti-tank TOE points (Case 19.8).
3. Units with ID `ee` and `ff` have a Breakdown Adjustment Rating of 1 Left.
