## Kampfgruppen HQ's (Basic Morale: That of the attached units; ID Code: b) — PDF p165
Prose-only formation — no unit table. A single ID Code applies to all Kampfgruppen HQ counters: **b**.

Footnotes / rules text:
- a = Kampfgruppen were *ad hoc* formations (brigade-level) of virtually every type and combination of units. They were formed for specific missions, and reflected the incredible German administrative/organizational flexibility. German units rarely stayed in division formation in Africa, and the use of Kampfgruppen was prevalent. The Kampfgruppen headquarters listed below are a random selection of actual groups formed during the campaign: Gruppe Badde, Gruppe Bach (*Bach*), Gruppe Dedeking, Kampfgruppe Fischer, Kampfgruppe Graf (*Graf*), Kampfgruppe Kaiser, Kampfgruppe Knabe (*Knabe*), Kampfgruppe Kost, Gruppe Marcks (*Marcks*), Kampfgruppe Menny, Kampfgruppe Mitte, Kampfgruppe Pfeiffer (*Pfeiffer*), Gruppe Schwerin (*Schwrn*), and Gruppe Voss. These units may be formed and disbanded at will, within the restrictions listed below. If the Axis Player wishes to form more Kampfgruppen than he has battlegroup counters, he may do so.
- 1. A Kampfgruppe is formed simply by attaching a German unit to it, thereby creating the unit.
- 2. The Kampfgruppe counter must be removed (disbanded) when detaching the final German unit (elimination through combat does not apply). In such a situation any remaining Italian units must be detached and any remaining anti-tank TOE Strength Points assigned to the headquarters become replacement points.
- 3. The German Player may never attach/assign more than two artillery-*type* units to a Kampfgruppe unless it is either the 1st or 2nd Afrika Artillery Regiment headquarters (see the German non-Divisional artillery units sheet).
- 4. No more than a total of three Italian units may be attached to all of the German Kampfgruppen in play at any time.
