## Chart notes (verified from page 2)
1. From the 1/39 Game-Turn, `ww` reconnaissance units may be upgraded (Case 20.5).
2. From the 1/75 Game-Turn, all Italian Battle Groups and certain HQs may be augmented by anti-tank TOE points (Case 19.8).
3. `vv` = Autoblinda 41, `ww` = Autoblinda 40; both have BAR 1 Left.
4. Units deployed/arriving with Heavy AA TOE points use 75/46 AA guns (see Italian Tank & Gun chart).
5. Certain ID-code units only have an Armor Protection Rating when truck-transported (treated as mounted in halftracks, Case 14.88).
