# §36.53 Allied Off-Map Air Facilities Chart

Transcription of the §36.53 chart (desktop screenshot, 2026-09-10). These are the Commonwealth
off-map air facilities — the Suez Canal-zone boxes plus Ethiopia — each reached via an on-map
entry/exit hex. "Maximum Squadrons" is the facility's printed SGSU capacity (§36.5).

| Entry/Exit Hex | Air Facility | Distance to Entry/Exit Hex | Maximum Squadrons |
|---|---|---|---|
| E4033 | Port Said\* | 12 | 3 |
| E3433 | Abu Seier | 7 | 9 |
| E3433 | Ismailia | 9 | 9 |
| E1833 | Fayid | 11 | 9 |
| E1833 | Deversoir | 12 | 9 |
| E1833 | Kabrit | 13 | 9 |
| E0127 | Ethiopia† | 60 | Unlimited |

\* Port Said is a **Flying Boat Basin** (§36.3 — flying boats only).
† **Ethiopia:** no planes may be based here until the first OpStage of **Game-Turn 35**. Distance 60,
capacity Unlimited. (East Africa — not a "N. African" off-map facility for the §34.82C test.)

**Notes for the model:**
- These are all **Commonwealth** off-map facilities (the Axis off-map bases are the §43.0
  Tripoli/Tunisia boxes and Italy/Sicily/Crete, handled separately).
- The six Canal-zone boxes (Port Said, Abu Seier, Ismailia, Fayid, Deversoir, Kabrit) are the
  Commonwealth **N. African off-map** facilities referenced by §34.82C; Ethiopia is East African,
  unlimited, and time-gated, so it is excluded from that rule's "maximum planes possibly based"
  calculation.
- Printed-capacity sum of the six N. African boxes = 3 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 9 = **48 squadrons**.
